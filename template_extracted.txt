Instructions for Preparing the Report
Use this template for micro projects across Computer Engineering courses. Adapt technical subsections to the nature of the course and project.
Replace all prompts in square brackets or blank fields with project-specific content. Delete guidance text before final submission.
Use clear headings, numbered figures/tables, readable diagrams, and consistent citation style. Ensure all screenshots and results are your own or appropriately acknowledged.
The report should describe work actually completed. Do not claim implementation, testing, deployment, or results that have not been demonstrated.
Include a Sustainability / SDG analysis for every project, even when the project is not explicitly an environmental application. Explain the project's positive, negative, or neutral impacts with evidence.
Follow the word count, page limit, group size, and submission format specified by the course faculty.
